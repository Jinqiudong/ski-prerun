# Agent guide

Product contract: [venture-lab `product-incubator/products/48/PRODUCT.md`](https://github.com/Jinqiudong/venture-lab/blob/main/product-incubator/products/48/PRODUCT.md). Idea: [venture-lab #48](https://github.com/Jinqiudong/venture-lab/issues/48).

## Product principles (do not break)
- The job is route familiarity for a real ski day. Every feature should help someone know where they are, where a lift or run goes, and how to get from A to B at their level.
- Real data stays real: run names, difficulties, lifts and terrain come from OpenStreetMap and terrain tiles via `tools/bake.py`. Never invent runs, lifts or connections; mark gaps instead.
- Never route a rider over runs harder than their chosen level without saying so.
- Look: stylized realism — real proportions, soft snow, long blue shadows, boxed hand-drawn paper UI. Not chibi. 3D facilities are semi-realistic (see `models/facilities/blender/README.md`).
- Icons come from the owner's painted library (`assets/icons/README.md`); ask the owner for missing ones.
- Snowboard only. Every rider is built from the character templates (`src/rider/character.js`): the rider page has the four friends and the ones you dress; the ride draws the same rider in the templates' light version with their ride clips (`src/rider/boarder.js`). The one exception: a rider may ride as one of the resort animals (`ch.animal`; `src/rider/animal-core.js` lists them, `animal.js` puts one on a board, procedural, no human clips).
- It must run in a phone browser from a static site (GitHub Pages), no build step. Shared trips use Supabase (`tools/supabase.sql`) by the owner's choice; friends never need an account.
- Always show the OpenStreetMap attribution and the "use the official trail map on the mountain" note.

## Layout
- Pages (their URLs are shared, keep them): `index.html` → `start.html` (rider, map, trips, log), `mountain/` (a resort as a miniature: pick a base and a lift), `look.html` (the ride), `sound.html` (the sound lab: every sound and music track, synth next to real recordings, with picks to send us).
- `src/core/` — shared: `i18n.js` (copy, English, avatars), `nav.js`, `route.js` (route graph and planner, no three.js; tested), `skilog.js` (what you've skied), `look.js` (my avatar and rider, as friends on a shared trip see them), `party.js` (一起滑, the party: friends on a shared trip together on every page over the Realtime channel `party:<trip>`: presence, invites, 去找他, 跟着他, 一起坐, 叫他来, voice off the slopes; its entry and notifications are the owner's capybara peeking over a snowy ledge on the screen's edge, holding up a sign; started by start.js, mountain.js and ride.js), `party-core.js` (its rules with no DOM: message checks, who's live, follow resolution, 一起坐 timing and seats, the per-page pet spots; tested), `transition.js` (the short scenes between pages: the flight to a resort, the lift ticket, the drop-in card; its `COPY` pool is where the owner adds lines).
- `src/start/` — the first screen: `start.js`, `collection.js` (the Colorado map), `resort-tiles.js` (resort tiles as dioramas, animals), `pets.js` (the tile animals as desk pets: lift, drop, tap, emotes), `intro.js` (the opening), `trips.js` (trips and sharing), `memory.js` (the log: a deck of group photos, one per ski day, filtered by season), `season.js` (ski days by season; tested), `photo.js` (draws a day's group photo in 2D from the owner's crayon pose sheets; new poses are a file and one line in `POSES`), `entrance.js` (the rider page's snow world: the slope, the painting on a ring round the camera, and the entrances' routes, traced from clips by `character.js` `trace`). The rider page is the whole screen in the snow: poses (loops) and entrances (出场, `ch.fav` the one played on opening) along the bottom, and one wardrobe behind 换装 (a rail and a sheet; the tabs step aside): what you wear is `st.wear` in localStorage `prerun` (`{ ch, from, name, stance, dirty }`), read by `src/core/look.js` and `boarder.js` `myRider`; saved outfits stay in `st.riders`. Tricks are the ride's (its trick wheel), not the rider page's.
- `src/rider/` — `character.js` (templates: body, fit, parts, colours, moves, presets), `boarder.js` (the ride's rider: a template's light version on its board, dressed from a look; its clips and poses, for me and for friends riding together), `animal-core.js` (the animal riders: names, toy sizes, a look's animal checked; tested), `animal.js` (an animal on a board: the ride's poses for it and its figure on the rider page; thumbnails by `tools/render_animal_thumbs.mjs`).
- `src/mountain/` — `mountain.js` (the resort page: the whole mountain, then a flight down to a base to pick a lift), `aerial.js` (the resort from the air in the start map's style, from `assets/aerial/<id>.jpg`: the resort page's scene, and the ride's lift top as a view of its own).
- `src/ride/` — `ride.js` (the ride: up the lift, the top, the run down, guide, maps, tricks), `board.js` (board physics, tracks, spray) and `edge-core.js` (the board on its edge as numbers: the fall line, gravity by the board's angle to it, the brake from the board turned across the way you go, 45° none to 90° full; progress in localStorage `prerunPractice`; tested) and `practice.js` (it run in the ride, `look.html?practice=1`: the world built round the hill's chairlift, the board steered freely, the step card, the gauges, the resort's animal as the coach; entered from the resort page's 去宝宝坡练习 pin and the base's 宝宝坡 card), `guide-core.js` (the guide animal's rules with no DOM: which animal, what it says, keeping 15–25 m ahead, waiting, holding at a turn-off, coming back; - `src/styles/` — `paper.css` (the shared paper theme, loaded after each page's own sheet) and one sheet per page (`<page>.css`, plus `<page>-paper.css` for overrides that load after the theme).
- `assets/` — icons, animals, the party pet (`party/`: the capybara's expressions, its sign, ledge pieces; cut from the owner's sheet by `tools/cut_party_art.py`), riders, textures, paintings, tiles, the resorts from the air (`aerial/<id>.jpg` and their bounds in `data/aerial.json`, from `tools/bake_tiles.py --aerial`), audio (`audio/music/`: the manifest; `audio/lab/`: the lab's CC0 recordings and their manifest, made by `tools/lab_audio.mjs`; `audio/game/`: the recordings the ride plays, made by `tools/game_audio.mjs`; CC0 or CC-BY only). `data/` — baked resorts (`<id>.json`, `<id>-far.json`), where runs and lifts fall on each watercolour (`vista/<id>.json`, `tools/bake_vista_lines.mjs`), `resorts.json`, region, drives, hikes, flats, rider templates.

- `tools/` — the data and art pipeline (`bake*.py`, `fetch_resort.py`, `lift_tags.py`, `build_animals.py`, `cut_party_art.py`, `watercolor.py`, `render_thumbs.mjs` (the wardrobe's accessory pictures, `assets/riders/thumbs/`), `supabase.sql`) and `check.mjs`.
- `tests/` — `node --test` unit tests. `docs/art/DECISIONS.md` — art decisions the owner made.

## Conventions
- ES modules loaded straight from the pages with an import map for three.js 0.170 (CDN). Cache-bust with `?v=N` on the import or link and bump it when a file changes.
- Paths to `assets/` and `data/` inside modules are relative to the page that loads them, not the module.
- Code, docs, commits and PRs are in English. UI copy is Simplified Chinese (English through `src/core/i18n.js`).

## Workflow
- Branch → PR → review → merge; `main` deploys to Pages. CI runs `npm run check` (every literal path resolves) and `npm test`.
- Before merging: `npm run check`, `npm test`, and play the flow in a phone-sized headless browser (start → resort → base → lift → top → run) with no console errors.
